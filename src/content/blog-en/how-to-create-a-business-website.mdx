Short answer: there are three ways to get a business website. You can build it yourself in a site builder like Wix: a few days of your time and a subscription of roughly 60–200 PLN a month. Or you can order a WordPress site or a site written from scratch. I build both at the same price: a one-page site from 1,990 PLN net, a business site with up to 5 pages 2,990 PLN net, plus 23% VAT.

Below is each route in turn: what you get, what you pay upfront and monthly, and who it suits.

## Three routes to a business website

| | Site builder (Wix, Tilda, Squarespace) | WordPress | Site written from scratch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who builds it | you | a contractor or you | a contractor |
| Upfront cost | 0 PLN plus your time | typically 1,500–4,000 PLN on the Polish market | typically 2,000–8,000 PLN on the Polish market |
| Monthly cost | subscription, approx. 60–200 PLN | server and domain approx. 15–60 PLN, plus updates | server and domain approx. 15–60 PLN |
| Who edits the content | you, in the builder | you, in the admin panel | the contractor |
| How long it takes | a few days of your work | 1–3 weeks | 1–3 weeks |

## Site builders: Wix, Tilda, Squarespace

You put the site together from ready-made blocks in your browser. The server, the padlock next to the address and updates are handled by the provider.

<GreenRed
  green="Pros: you can go live in a few days without hiring anyone, and the upfront cost is low. The provider takes care of everything technical."
  red="Cons: the site is rented. Stop paying the subscription and it disappears, and you can't move it to another provider. Many sites look alike because they come from the same templates. You write the text, pick the photos and set everything up yourself, usually in the evenings."

**Who it suits:** a start, when you're testing a business idea or your budget is very small. Just work out the subscription over a few years: 100 PLN a month is 6,000 PLN over five years.

## WordPress

The most popular website system in the world. It has an admin panel where you change text and photos or add posts yourself.

<GreenRed
  green="Pros: you edit the content yourself without calling the contractor. It's easy to find people who know WordPress. There's a plugin for almost everything."
  red="Cons: the system and plugins need regular updates. A neglected WordPress site is the most common target of break-ins on small business websites. Any plugin can break after an update."
/>

**Who it suits:** businesses that change something on the site every week: a menu, offers, completed jobs, news. With me a WordPress site costs the same as one written from scratch. Care for it is 249 PLN net a month, updates included.

## A site written from scratch

A website built around your business, in my case with Next.js. It has no plugins and no admin panel exposed to the internet, so there's nothing to patch every week. It usually loads fast, because it doesn't load anything it doesn't need.

There's one downside: no click-to-edit panel, so changes are made by the contractor. With my Website Care plan (199 PLN net a month) you get one hour of changes a month.

**Who it suits:** businesses that just need a site that works, looks good and brings in customers from Google, with content that changes a few times a year.

## 6 questions to ask a contractor before paying a deposit

**1. Who will the domain be registered to?** There's only one good answer: your business.

**2. What's included in the price, and what's extra?** Ask about text, photos, revisions, the domain and the server. Get the answer in writing.

**3. What will I pay in the second year?** Server, domain, care. A specific amount, not "we'll see".

**4. Who edits the content after launch, and what does it cost?** Will there be an admin panel, or does the contractor make changes, and at what hourly rate?

**5. Can I see 2–3 live websites you've built?** Real addresses, not just pictures. Open them on your phone.

**6. What does the process look like, and when do I pay?** A good contractor has it worked out: quote, contract, deposit, design, revisions, launch, final payment.

<Callout type="warning">
**Keep looking if the contractor:**
- promises first place in Google within a month,
- has no live websites to show,
- wants to register the domain "under their name, it's simpler",
- doesn't ask what you do or who your customers are,
- won't put the arrangements in writing.
</Callout>

## FAQ

**How long does it take to create a business website?**

In a site builder, a few days of your own work. With a contractor, usually 1–3 weeks. With me, 7–14 working days from the day I have all the materials: logo, text, photos and business details.

**Which route should a small business choose?**

Testing an idea on a very small budget: a site builder. Changing something on the site every week: WordPress. A site that should just work and bring in customers, with content that rarely changes: a site written from scratch.

**Is WordPress better than Wix?**

Neither is better for everyone. Wix is simpler, but the site stays on the platform and you pay the subscription for as long as you use it. WordPress gives you more freedom and the site is yours, but it needs regular updates.

**Do I have to pay for a domain and server on top of the website?**

Yes. A .pl domain usually costs 50–150 PLN a year on renewal, and a server for a business site 150–600 PLN a year. Site builders include them in the subscription. With me, the first year is included in the price of the website. Details are in [how much does website maintenance cost](/en/blog/website-maintenance-cost).
