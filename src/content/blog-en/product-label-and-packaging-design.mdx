## A label sells before the customer picks the product up

At a fair, in a local products shop or on Instagram you have about three seconds. In that time the customer isn't judging the taste, but whether the product looks worth its price.

## A product range: plan it from the start

The most common mistake small producers make: one label is designed, and six months later a second flavour "in a similar style" is designed from scratch. After a year there are five products on the shelf that don't look like one brand.

It's better to design a **range template** from the start: a fixed layout, logo and typeface, with only the colour and variant name changing. Every new flavour is then a swap of a few elements, not a new design.

Have several variants? Tell me how many and I'll quote the whole range together, instead of each label separately.

## Don't have a logo yet?

A label without a logo works, but a range of products without a consistent mark struggles to build recognition. If you plan to sell more than one product, start with a logo. After that labels are quicker and cheaper to design, because colours and typefaces are already set.

## When you need packaging, not just a label

A label is enough when the product already has its container: a jar, a bottle, a tube. Packaging (a box, a bag, a sleeve for a box) makes sense when:

- you sell the product as a gift and how the box looks is part of the purchase,
- the product is delicate or has several parts that need packing together,
- you ship parcels and want opening them to be an experience, not just unpacking.

For packaging I outsource the printing, as it needs a cutting die and a different machine. I prepare the design together with a file for the specific print shop, so there are no surprises when the box is assembled.

## FAQ

**How much does a product label design cost?**

With me from 180 PLN for a product label of up to two sides. On the market usually 150–600 PLN, depending on whether it's a simple label or a design with an illustration.

**How much does printing labels in a small run cost?**

With me 50 pieces 89 PLN, 100 pieces 149 PLN, 200 pieces 229 PLN. Waterproof vinyl, needed for jars and bottles, adds 25%.

**How much does packaging design cost?**

Designing a box, bag or sleeve starts at 400 PLN with me. I outsource packaging printing and quote it separately, as it depends on the cutting die, material and run size.

**Can I order just a few dozen labels?**

Yes. I print in-house, so the smallest run is 50 pieces. It's a good way to test a label at a fair before ordering more.

**What information must be on a food product label?**

Among other things: the product name, ingredients, weight or volume, producer details and the use-by or best-before date. Confirm the full list for your product category with whoever handles your formalities.

**Can a jar label be paper?**

Better not. A jar taken out of the fridge gets covered in condensation, the paper soaks it up and the label peels off. For products that come into contact with moisture, choose vinyl.
