Not all designers are equal. One will make a logo that works for 10 years, another will hand over a low-resolution Canva file and disappear. The trouble is, at the talking stage they both sound similar.

Here's how to tell them apart. Prices in PLN, net.

The portfolio is the most important indicator: more important than the price, more important than Facebook reviews.

## Questions to ask before hiring

Don't only ask about the price. Ask:

- **What files will I get?** You should receive vectors (AI, EPS, SVG), a print PDF and PNGs for the web. No vectors is a problem for years.
- **How many revisions are included?** Good studios give a specific number, not "we'll revise until you're happy" (a trap for both sides)
- **Is the design made from scratch?** Ask directly whether they use stock elements or AI generators
- **Who keeps the copyright?** Once paid, the copyright should pass to you

## A local or a remote designer?

Many business owners in Rzeszów prefer to meet in person before hiring someone. I understand that, but in my experience the quality of a design doesn't depend on whether the designer sits 3 kilometres from your office.

It depends on whether the designer knows how to ask questions.

A good brief can be done over the phone, on Zoom or by message. Even a meeting over coffee won't fix bad communication. I work with clients from all over Poland and the process is the same, just without wasting time travelling.

The one situation where being local genuinely helps: when you need a physical print sample before ordering the full run. In Rzeszów you can simply drop by and see the print in person. And if you're new in town and more comfortable in English, a designer who can work with you in English saves a lot of misunderstandings.

## Red flags that should stop you

Before you sign a contract or transfer a deposit, think twice if:

- The designer doesn't ask about your business, competitors and target audience, and designs by guesswork
- They promise a finished design in 24 hours (unless it's a simple business card)
- There's no contract, just arrangements over Messenger
- The price is "negotiable" with no justification
- The portfolio is empty or contains only work "for friends"

## FAQ

**How much does a good graphic designer in Rzeszów cost?**

A basic logo is 500–1,500 PLN, a logo with a brand book 1,000–3,000 PLN, and a full brand identity 1,500–5,000 PLN. With me a logo starts at 890 PLN, a logo with a mini brand book costs 1,190 PLN, and the Marka Start package 1,490 PLN. Designing a single business card or flyer comes to 100–300 PLN. Offers under 200 PLN for a logo are usually a generator or a ready template.

**How do I recognise a good designer?**

By a portfolio of real work rather than just mock-ups, by them asking questions before they start designing, by a specific number of included revisions and by a clear statement that the copyright passes to you once paid.

**Does the designer have to be from my town?**

No. The quality of a design depends on whether the designer can gather a good brief, not on distance. Location helps in one situation: when you want to see a physical print sample before ordering the whole run.

**What files should I get at the end of the project?**

Vectors (AI, EPS or SVG), a print-ready PDF and PNGs for web use, in colour variants and in versions for light and dark backgrounds. A JPG or PNG alone isn't enough: without vectors you can't enlarge the mark for a shop sign or billboard.

**Who owns the rights to the design?**

Once the job is paid, the copyright should pass to you, and it's worth having that in writing. If the contractor avoids this or talks about a time-limited licence, it's a warning sign: you may have problems registering a trademark.
