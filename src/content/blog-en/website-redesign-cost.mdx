## Fix it or start over: a simple rule

A redesign doesn't always mean "everything from scratch". Sometimes two hours of work is enough. Sometimes patching would cost more than a new site and still look patched.

## Will I lose my Google position after a redesign?

It's the most common question, and a fair one. If someone builds a new site and simply deletes the old one, Google keeps hitting addresses that no longer exist for a while. A customer from the search results sees an error.

That's why I redirect every old address to its new equivalent. For example: the old page `/oferta-uslug.html` now leads to `/uslugi`. Google gets the signal that the page has moved, not disappeared.

Most important: don't change your domain during the redesign unless you have to. The old domain has its history with Google. If a change is necessary, see [how to choose a domain name for your business](/en/blog/how-to-choose-a-domain-name).

## WordPress or a site written from scratch

With a redesign it's worth asking yourself one question: do you want to change the content yourself, or would you rather I did it?

If you change a menu, offers or add completed work every week, WordPress with an admin panel will suit you better. If the site changes twice a year, a site written from scratch is enough. Both cost the same with me: 2,990 PLN net for a site with up to 5 pages. The differences are in [WordPress or a custom-coded website](/en/blog/wordpress-or-custom-website).

**Can I keep my old domain?**

Yes, and it's usually worth it. The new site goes live at the same address, and I redirect the old pages to the new ones. Customers and Google end up where they always did.

**Will you move the content from my old site?**

Yes. I take what works: service descriptions, photos, reviews, contact details. Text that's outdated or too long, I shorten and improve.

**I don't have the passwords to my old site. What now?**

Access to the domain is enough. If you don't know where the domain is registered, I'll help find out. A new site can be built without access to the old one too, because I can copy the content from what's visible in the browser.
