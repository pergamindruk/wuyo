Short answer: at a large online print shop in Poland, printing 100 business cards costs 40–90 PLN, but only for runs of 250 and up. For a small run with a design from scratch, the real cost is 300–450 PLN.

Those two numbers describe two different services, and that's exactly why comparing them leads to misunderstandings.

Below I break it down so you know what you're paying for and where you can genuinely save. Prices in PLN, net.

## Why small runs can be poor value

Large online print shops work on shared sheets. They collect orders from many customers on one sheet, print once and split the cost. That's why they have good prices at 1000 pieces, and why they often won't take orders for 50 at all, or charge the same as for 250.

I print in-house on A3+ equipment, so I start at 50, not 250. Want 50 business cards for a trade fair tomorrow? I'll make them. It's not a marketing gimmick, just a consequence of not having to wait for a sheet to fill up.

## Print only, or design and print?

This is the first decision and the biggest price difference.

**You have a ready file:** you pay for printing only. The condition is that the file is prepared correctly: 3 mm bleed, CMYK colours, text converted to outlines, 300 dpi resolution. Without that, the print shop either sends the file back or prints something you didn't expect.

**You don't have a design:** you pay for design and print. Designing a business card usually costs 100–250 PLN if you already have a logo. With me a single-sided card design is 160 PLN, double-sided 240 PLN, and together with printing it comes out cheaper than separately. If you don't have a logo, that's a different conversation and budget; I wrote about it in [how much does a logo cost](/blog/ile-kosztuje-logo).

## What really affects the price

In order from biggest impact to smallest.

- **Run size:** the more, the cheaper per card, but the total rises only slightly
- **Design:** from scratch or adapting an existing one? That's a 100–200 PLN difference
- **Paper weight:** 250 g vs 350 g is about 10–15% of the print price
- **Finish:** matt and gloss are usually included; soft-touch film, spot varnish or gold foil add 50–150%
- **Double-sided:** 2/2 printing costs roughly twice as much as 1/0
- **Unusual format or shape:** rounded corners, square cards and contour cutting raise the price
- **Deadline:** 24-hour express usually adds around 30%

## What not to skimp on

<Callout type="warning">
**Three savings that cost more than they save:**

- **Paper under 300 g:** the card bends in a wallet and looks like a discount coupon. The price difference is a few złoty across the whole run.
- **A design from a free generator:** the card will look familiar, because hundreds of other businesses have the same template. And there's almost never a vector version for print.
- **Printing without a proof for unusual colours:** navy, deep black and intense green come out differently on paper than on screen. With 500 cards that's an expensive lesson.
</Callout>

## FAQ

**How much do 100 business cards cost?**

Printing 100 double-sided cards on 350 g paper costs 40–90 PLN depending on the supplier. With a design from scratch, 150–350 PLN. With me, 100 cards with design are 349 PLN, and printing 100 from a ready file is 159 PLN.

**How much does it cost to print business cards from my own file?**

For small runs with me: 50 cards 99 PLN, 100 cards 159 PLN, 300 cards 299 PLN. At a large online print shop printing can be cheaper, but usually from 250 pieces up and with waiting for delivery.

**What paper should I choose for business cards?**

My standard is 300 g gloss: stiff and makes colours pop. At large print shops you'll more often find 350 g, matt or gloss; matt looks calmer and is easier to write on with a pen. Below 300 g a business card is too flimsy.

**What do I need to prepare to order?**

Your logo in vector format if you have it, and the details that go on the card: name, job title, phone, email, website. The rest is my job. How to write it down sensibly, I describe in [how to write a design brief](/blog/jak-napisac-brief-dla-projektanta).

**Will I own the print files?**

Yes. Once the order is paid you get a print-ready PDF, so you can reprint wherever you like. I don't lock anyone in.
