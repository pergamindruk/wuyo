Here's how logo design works with me, step by step: a brief, three directions to choose from, revisions until you approve, and finally the files and rights. Prices start at 890 PLN net, plus 23% VAT.

I'm writing this because most people order a logo for the first time in their lives. You don't know what will happen, when you need to say something, or what you're paying for. Below is the whole process, stage by stage.

## Logo design step by step: 6 stages

In short:

1. Brief: a conversation and questions about your business.
2. Quote and offer.
3. Contract and a 50% deposit.
4. Three directions to choose from.
5. Revisions to the chosen direction.
6. Handover: files, rights, final invoice.

Now one by one.

## Step 1. The brief: the most important stage

Before I draw the first line, I need to know who you are. I ask about your industry, customers, competitors and where the logo will appear: on a van, a business card, a website, T-shirts.

A good brief means the first proposal is close to what you need. A weak brief means shooting in the dark and three rounds of changes nobody enjoys.

An example: "I want something modern" tells me nothing. "I do roofing, my customers are 50+, the logo will mostly be on a tarpaulin and the side of a van" is something to work with.

You don't have to write an essay. A ready [design brief template](/en/blog/how-to-write-a-design-brief) has questions you just fill in.

## Steps 2 and 3. Quote, offer, contract

After the brief I know what we're doing. You get an offer with a net price, the scope and exactly what you'll get at the end. No asterisks or small print.

Then a contract and a 50% deposit. You pay the rest only after approving the design. It's fair to both sides: I know you're serious, and you don't pay in full for something you haven't seen yet.

## Step 4. Three directions to choose from

This is where the design starts. I show you one main concept and two other directions. Not twenty variants of the same mark, but three different ideas for your business.

Why? Because with twenty proposals you can't decide. With three you see the difference straight away and know which direction "is you".

When choosing, don't just look at a big screen. Shrink the logo to the size of an icon on a phone and picture it on a business card. A logo that looks great on a monitor but disappears on a pen is a weak logo.

<Callout type="info">
It works best when one person decides on the direction. When the whole family and a business partner judge the logo, everyone wants something different and the design gets diluted.
</Callout>

## Step 5. Revisions: until it's right, but within the chosen direction

You choose one direction and we refine it. Revisions are included and I work until you approve. A darker colour, bolder letters, the mark closer to the name: that's a normal part of the work.

I'll say this up front: if after choosing a direction you change your mind and want a completely different logo, that's a new quote, because we're starting the design from scratch.

How long does it take? A logo on its own takes me 7–10 working days from brief approval. Most depends on how quickly you come back with comments. One day of silence on your side is one more day.

## Step 6. Handover: files and rights

At the end you get a package with the logo in three versions: horizontal, vertical and single-colour. It contains files for print and for the web, meaning vectors and PNGs with a transparent background. What each one is and which file to send to the print shop is covered in [what logo files you should get from a designer](/en/blog/logo-files-from-designer).

Once paid in full, the economic copyright to the logo passes to you together with the source files. The contract lists the fields of use (print, web, vehicle graphics, clothing and more) and gives consent to modifications. So you can use and change the logo without asking my permission. Why this matters and what the contract must contain is explained in [logo copyright](/en/blog/logo-copyright).

## FAQ

**How much does logo design cost?**

With me from 890 PLN net, plus 23% VAT. The price includes the logo in horizontal, vertical and single-colour versions, revisions to the chosen direction and the transfer of rights. With a mini brand book it's 1,190 PLN net.

**How long does the logo design process take, from brief to files?**

A logo on its own takes 7–10 working days from brief approval. It takes longer if I wait a long time for comments or a decision.

**How many logo proposals will I get?**

One main concept and two other directions. You choose one and we refine it until you approve.

**What if I don't like any of the directions?**

Say plainly what doesn't fit. Usually the cause is in the brief: something was missing or we understood it differently. That's why I put so much attention into the first stage.
