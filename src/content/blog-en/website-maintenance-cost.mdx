Short answer: running a simple business website costs from about 20 PLN a month if you do everything yourself, to roughly 200 PLN a month if someone looks after it for you.

The difference doesn't come from the server itself. You pay for whether someone takes care of backups, updates and small changes, or whether you do it yourself on a free evening.

Below I break down what this cost is made of, what's mandatory and where businesses overpay. Prices in PLN, net.

## What website maintenance consists of

There are two kinds of costs. Mandatory ones, without which the site stops working. And optional ones that save you time and stress.

| Item | Approximate cost | Mandatory? |
|---------|--------------------|--------------|
| `.pl` domain (website address) | 50–150 PLN / year | Yes |
| Server (hosting) for a business website | 150–600 PLN / year | Yes |
| SSL certificate (padlock in the browser) | 0 PLN, usually included with the server | Yes |
| Backups | 0–200 PLN / year | Practically yes |
| Updates and security | Your time or a care plan | With WordPress, yes |
| Small content changes | Your time or 100 PLN / h | No |

So the mandatory costs come to about 200–750 PLN a year, or 17–60 PLN a month. Anything above that is paying for someone's time.

<Callout type="info">
Domains are often on offer for a few złoty in the first year. Check the **renewal** price: that's what you'll pay every year.
</Callout>

## WordPress costs more to run than it seems

A WordPress site consists of the core plus a dozen or so plugins. Each of them gets updates, and an out-of-date plugin is the most common way someone breaks into a small business website.

That's why with WordPress, care isn't an extra. Someone has to check updates every week or two and make sure nothing broke afterwards. If nobody does, the site works until it stops.

The websites I build are made with Next.js. They have no plugins and no admin panel exposed to the internet, so there's nothing to patch every week. Maintenance comes down to the domain, server and backups. I describe the differences between technologies in [how to create a business website](/en/blog/how-to-create-a-business-website).

## How much does website care cost

Care is a subscription where someone takes over the technical side for you. On the Polish market the typical rate for a small business is around 150–300 PLN a month. Cheaper offers usually cover just the server, more expensive ones also include work on Google visibility.

Before you sign, check three things:

<CheckItem>How many hours of changes are included: "unlimited small changes" usually means arguing about what counts as small</CheckItem>
<CheckItem>Whether backups are made automatically and can be restored</CheckItem>
<CheckItem>What the notice period is</CheckItem>
<CheckItem ok={false}>A one- or two-year contract with no early cancellation</CheckItem>
<CheckItem ok={false}>The domain registered to the contractor, not to your business</CheckItem>

The last point is the most important. The domain is your business's address on the internet. If it's registered to someone else, you can lose it when you part ways, together with your position in Google.

## Where businesses overpay

<Callout type="warning">
**The three most common cases:**

- **A "business" server for a five-page website:** you pay for power you won't use. A business website without a shop doesn't need the most expensive plan.
- **Care with unlimited hours, but in practice no changes:** you pay every month, and every request gets "that's not a small change".
- **Several subscriptions at once:** domain, server, backups and certificate all billed separately. Worth gathering the invoices once a year and adding them up.
</Callout>

## When to maintain the website yourself

Maintaining it yourself makes sense if the site has no plugins to update, you rarely make changes and you can log in to your domain and server panels. Then you only pay the mandatory costs from the table above.

It doesn't make sense if the site runs on WordPress and you don't have time to check updates every two weeks. Then saving 150 PLN a month usually ends with one repair costing several hundred.

If the website is meant to win customers from Google, the technical side alone isn't enough. A Google Business Profile often brings more calls than the website; how to set it up, I describe in [Google Business Profile for a small business](/en/blog/google-business-profile-small-business).

## FAQ

**How much does website maintenance cost per month?**

The mandatory costs, the domain and server, come to about 17–60 PLN a month. With care, where someone looks after backups, security and small changes, usually 150–300 PLN a month. With me Website Care costs 199 PLN a month.

**How much does a domain cost per year?**

A `.pl` domain usually costs 50–150 PLN a year at renewal. The first year is often cheaper on offer, so compare renewal prices, not first registration.

**Do I have to pay for website care?**

No. Only the domain and server are mandatory. Care saves time and adds security: with WordPress it's practically necessary, with a plugin-free site backups alone are enough.

**What's included in website care?**

It depends on the provider, so always check the scope in writing. With me Website Care covers the server, domain, backups, security and 1 hour of small changes a month for 199 PLN.

**Who should the domain be registered to?**

To your business. A domain registered to the contractor means the risk of losing your website address, your Google position and your email addresses on that domain when you change providers.

**How much does changing text on a website cost?**

With a care plan small changes are usually included up to an agreed limit. Beyond the limit or without a subscription you pay for time: with me 100 PLN an hour, with the cost given before work starts.
