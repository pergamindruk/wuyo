## Why two logos at the same price are not the same

With a logo you don't pay for the file. A file can be made in an hour.

You pay for someone checking what your competitors look like, so your mark isn't the third identical one in town. For someone drawing several directions and throwing out the weaker ones before you ever see them. For someone checking how the logo behaves in a three-centimetre circle, on a black background and embroidered on a cap.

You can't see that in the price. You only see it when the logo has to be used somewhere, and it either works or you pay extra to patch it.

## What affects the price of a logo?

- **The designer's experience:** junior vs senior vs branding agency
- **Scope:** just a logo vs a full brand identity
- **Number of concepts:** 1 proposal vs 3 directions
- **Revisions:** how many rounds are included
- **Final files:** JPG only, or SVG/AI/PDF/PNG in every variant
- **Copyright:** a transfer vs a licence

## FAQ

**Does a logo have to be expensive?**

No. It has to be well thought out. A mark for 900–2,500 PLN from someone who shows you a portfolio and asks questions before drawing will serve you for years. A logo for 200 PLN usually gets replaced after a year, and you pay twice, plus replacing every material it has already appeared on.

**What goes into the price of a good logo?**

Time for research (who you are, who you're talking to, what the competition looks like), sketching concepts, the digital design, final files in many formats and the copyright transfer.

**Will I get vector files?**

Yes, and make sure you ask for them. SVG and AI/EPS are formats that let you print at any size without losing quality. PNG and JPG are only good for screens.

**How long does a logo take?**

It depends on how quickly you come back with comments, which usually matters more than the work itself. I deliver the Marka Start package in 7–10 working days from the deposit. A logo on its own takes 7–10 working days from the approved brief. The full scope and current prices are on the [logo design](/logo) page.

**How many concepts do I get to choose from?**

I show directions, not twenty variants of the same thing. You choose one and we refine it until it's right; revisions to the chosen concept are included. Multiplying proposals sounds good in an offer, but in practice it makes the decision harder and dilutes the work.

**Does the price include the copyright transfer?**

Yes. Once paid in full, the copyright to the mark passes to you together with the source files. You can do whatever you want with the logo without asking anyone's permission.

**Do I have to register my logo with the Patent Office?**

You don't have to. A logo is protected by copyright from the moment it's created. Registering a trademark gives stronger protection of the name and form in specific industries and involves separate official fees. Check current fees and scope with the Polish Patent Office (or the EUIPO for EU-wide protection) or a patent attorney.
