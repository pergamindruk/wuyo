## Who builds websites in Rzeszów?

Three types of business operate on the Rzeszów market:

**1. Large marketing agencies**
They serve corporations, have teams of several people and correspondingly high rates. A simple website for a small business often isn't their priority: it goes to a junior or gets subcontracted further.

**2. Freelancers and small studios**
You work directly with the person actually building your site. Faster contact, less bureaucracy, often a better price. The downside can be a lack of capacity for bigger projects.

**3. "Agencies" without a portfolio**
They work mainly through ads. They sell ready WordPress templates as "custom design" and disappear after the project. Avoid them.

## What to ask before signing a contract

Before you pay a deposit, ask:

- **Can I see working websites you've built?** Not mock-ups, not screenshots, but live addresses
- **What technology will the site use?** WordPress, Next.js, Webflow, or maybe Wix? Each has different consequences for speed and security
- **Who will own the domain and hosting when it's finished?** You should have full access
- **What does the price cover, and what costs extra?** Ask directly about text, photos, SEO, revisions
- **What does contact look like after handover?** Who helps when something stops working

If English is easier for you than Polish, also ask whether the contractor can work with you in English and whether the site can be bilingual.

## WordPress or Next.js: which to choose?

The most common question from small businesses is: "does it matter?" It does, a lot.

| Criterion | WordPress | Next.js |
|-----------|-----------|---------|
| Speed (PageSpeed) | 50–75/100 | 90–100/100 |
| Security | Needs updates every week | No exposed backend, very little to attack |
| Running costs | Hosting + premium plugins | Your hosting (depends on provider) |
| SEO | Good with plugins | Built in, better |

For a small local business that doesn't need an extensive CMS, Next.js is the better choice in the long run.

## FAQ

**How much does a website cost in Rzeszów?**

A one-page business card site is 1,800–3,500 PLN, a business website with several pages 2,900–7,000 PLN and an online shop 5,000–20,000 PLN. Differences within these ranges come mainly from the number of pages, the amount of content to prepare and whether the design is made from scratch or on a ready template.

**WordPress or Next.js: which for a small business?**

Next.js wins on speed (90–100 points in PageSpeed versus 50–75) and security, because there's no database or plugins to attack. WordPress makes sense if you need an extensive editorial back end or a specific plugin. For a typical business website the price difference is small and the speed difference large.

**Who should own the domain and hosting?**

You. The domain registered to you, hosting on your account, files handed over at the end. If the contractor keeps the domain, you lose control of your own address when you change providers; it's one of the most common traps.

**How long does building a business website take?**

Usually 2–4 weeks from collecting the brief and materials. What takes the most time isn't coding but the client's text and photos: if you have them ready, everything goes much faster.

**Will I be able to edit the website myself?**

That's something to agree before starting. A content editing panel can be connected, or you can agree that the contractor makes small changes. Settle it at the quote stage, as adding a panel later means rebuilding part of the site.
