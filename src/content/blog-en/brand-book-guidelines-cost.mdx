**A brand book is a short manual for using your logo.** It says which versions it comes in, what colours it has (with codes for print and the web), which fonts to use, how much space to leave around the mark and what not to do with it. A simple brand book for a small business has 6 to 12 pages. With me a mini brand book costs 590 PLN on its own, or 300 PLN more when ordered with a logo design.

Without a brand book, every print shop, every designer and everyone running your social media makes the logo "by eye". A year later you have three shades of green and a logo stretched on your shop sign.

## What a brand book must include

<CheckItem>**Logo versions:** horizontal, vertical, the symbol alone (e.g. for an avatar), single-colour black and white.</CheckItem>
<CheckItem>**Colours with codes:** HEX and RGB for the web, CMYK for print, optionally Pantone for clothing and signs.</CheckItem>
<CheckItem>**Typefaces:** which font for headings, which for body text and where to get it (ideally free, e.g. from Google Fonts).</CheckItem>
<CheckItem>**Clear space:** how much empty space must stay around the logo so nothing overlaps it.</CheckItem>
<CheckItem>**Minimum size:** below what size the logo stops being legible, separately for print and screen.</CheckItem>
<CheckItem>**Logo on backgrounds:** how it looks on light, dark and on a photo.</CheckItem>
<CheckItem>**What not to do:** stretching, changing colours, shadows, rotating. Best shown with examples.</CheckItem>

That's the mini version, and it's all a small business needs. The full set of business materials is covered in [how much does a brand identity cost](/en/blog/brand-identity-cost), and the mark itself in [how much does a logo cost](/en/blog/logo-design-cost).

## Full brand book or mini brand book

A full brand book also covers business materials: business cards, letterhead, post templates, vehicle graphics, clothing, tone of voice. It sometimes runs to dozens of pages and is made for companies with several branches or many suppliers producing their materials.

## When a brand book comes in handy

- You're ordering a shop sign, vehicle graphics or clothing, and the supplier asks for "the colour in CMYK or Pantone" (the difference is explained in [CMYK vs RGB](/en/blog/cmyk-vs-rgb)).
- You're hiring someone to run your social media.
- You're building a website and want it to look like the rest of your materials.
- Your logo looks different on your business card, on Facebook and on your door.

If two of these apply to you, the brand book will pay for itself with your first print order, because you won't pay for colour corrections.

## FAQ

**How does a brand book differ from a brand identity?**

A brand book describes the logo itself and the basic rules (colours, fonts). A brand identity is the whole set of business materials: logo, business cards, letterhead, templates, signage. The brand book is part of the identity.

**How many pages does a brand book have?**

A mini brand book for a small business usually has 6 to 12 pages. Full brand books of big brands run to dozens, but a small business doesn't need that much.

**What format do I get the brand book in?**

As a PDF, together with the package of logo files: vectors (SVG, PDF, EPS) for print and PNG for the web, in colour, black and white versions.

**Can I order a brand book for a logo someone else designed?**

Yes. I need the logo's vector files. If you only have an image from the internet or a PNG, I first redraw the logo as a vector.
