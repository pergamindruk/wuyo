Short answer: I build a business website with up to 5 pages for 2,990 PLN net, the same price on WordPress or written from scratch. VAT of 23% is added to the price. The difference is in care after launch: 249 PLN net a month for WordPress, 199 PLN net for a site written from scratch.

I build both kinds of sites. I don't push either one. Each suits a different business, and the choice usually comes down to one question. I'll get to it in a moment.

## How they differ, in plain words

**WordPress** is a ready-made system with an editing panel. You log in like to your email, click on text, change it, save. You add a photo to the gallery, a new service, a news post. No calling the designer.

**A site written from scratch** (in my case with Next.js) is built from the ground up for your business. There's no click-to-edit panel. I make the changes: you email "change the service price to 150 PLN", and I do it.

Both sites can look the same. Both work on phones, have a form and a map, and are set up for Google. A customer visiting them won't see any difference.

## The one question that decides it

I ask it of everyone who comes for a website:

**Do you want to change the content yourself, or would you rather I did it?**

If you have something new every week (a daily menu, new projects, available slots, announcements), choose WordPress. You won't have to wait for me with every small change.

If your website is a business card that changes twice a year (services, prices, contact details), choose a site written from scratch. You don't need a panel you won't use anyway.

## Why WordPress care costs more

WordPress consists of the system and plugins. A plugin is an add-on responsible for a specific thing: a form, a gallery, backups. The system and plugins get updates, often several a month.

Updates can't be postponed, because an old version is an easier target for break-ins. And sometimes something stops working after an update and has to be fixed. It's regular work that hardly exists with a site written from scratch. Hence the 50 PLN difference a month.

What you get in care:

- **WordPress Care, 249 PLN / month:** server, domain, WordPress and plugin updates, backups, security, an hour of small changes a month.
- **Website Care, 199 PLN / month:** server, domain, backups, security, an hour of small changes a month.

I always build WordPress sites with care. Without it, a year later you have a site with outdated plugins, and that's an open door for anyone looking for an easy target. More on costs after launch in [how much does website maintenance cost](/en/blog/website-maintenance-cost).

## Whose account and website it is

For WordPress I set up the server account **in your details**. I pay for the first year as part of the website price. You get the login details at handover.

From the second year the server and domain are covered by care, so you don't have to keep track of renewal invoices. If you ever cancel care, you take over those payments yourself. The website is still yours.

## FAQ

**Is a WordPress site cheaper?**

Not with me. A business website with up to 5 pages costs 2,990 PLN net both on WordPress and written from scratch. The difference is in care: 249 PLN net a month for WordPress, 199 PLN net for a site written from scratch.

**Can I change anything myself on a site written from scratch?**

There's no click-to-edit panel. I make small changes as part of care, and you get an hour a month. If you want to change content yourself and often, WordPress will suit you better.

**Can I have WordPress without care?**

I don't build WordPress sites without care. The system and plugins have to be updated regularly, and a neglected site is an easy target for break-ins. WordPress Care costs 249 PLN net a month and also covers the server and domain.

**Which is better for Google?**

I set up both sites for Google the same way: titles, descriptions, fast loading, working on phones. Your position depends more on content and your [Google Business Profile](/en/blog/google-business-profile-small-business) than on what the site is built with.
