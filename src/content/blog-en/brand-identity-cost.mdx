A logo isn't a brand. A brand is what people think of your business, and a brand identity is the set of rules that makes them see the same thing every time: on a business card, in an email, on your van and on Facebook.

**How much does a brand identity cost?** On the Polish market a basic set (logo, colours, typography, a few items and a brand book) is 1,500–4,500 PLN. With me the Marka Start package (logo, mini brand book, 150 printed business cards and an email signature) costs 1,490 PLN. Prices net.

Below is what exactly goes into it, how it compares with the market and where to start if you can't afford everything at once.

## What does a brand identity include?

**Core elements:**
- Logo: symbol + wordmark + versions (vertical/horizontal, colour/mono/white)
- Colour palette: main + supporting + codes (HEX, CMYK, Pantone)
- Typography: heading and body fonts + usage rules
- Layout grid: rules for arranging elements

**Items and applications:**
- Business card
- Letterhead / email signature
- Company folder / quote document
- Presentation templates
- Social media templates (Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook)
- Packaging (if relevant)
- Shop sign / roll-up / premises signage

**Brand book:**
- A PDF document with rules for using the logo
- Examples of correct and incorrect use
- The colour palette with codes
- Typefaces and typesetting rules

## Why a small business needs it

It's not about prestige. It's about customers not having to wonder whether they're dealing with the same business.

A consistent look:
- Builds trust before you even start talking
- Shortens the work on every new material, because you have ready templates and colours
- Sets you apart from competitors who change their look with every material
- Makes outsourcing easier: the print shop and designer get ready guidelines instead of guessing

<Callout type="info">
The simplest test: put your business card, a printed quote and your Facebook profile picture side by side. If the colours don't match and the logo is in three different proportions, you don't need a new logo, you need rules.
</Callout>

## How work on a brand identity goes

1. **Conversation and brief:** what you do, who you talk to, what sets you apart
2. **Research:** what the competition looks like, so your mark isn't the third identical one
3. **Logo design:** directions to choose from, then refining one
4. **Development:** colours, typefaces, application on materials
5. **Brand book:** the full set of files and usage rules
6. **Handover:** every format: SVG, PDF, PNG, JPG and source files

## FAQ

**How much does a brand identity for a small business cost?**

On the market 1,500–4,500 PLN for a basic set, an extended identity 4,500–9,000 PLN. With me a logo with a mini brand book costs 1,190 PLN, and the Marka Start package with business cards and an email signature 1,490 PLN.

**Do I need everything at once?**

No. You can start with a logo and build up the identity gradually. What matters is settling at least the colours and typography at the start: they're the foundation of consistency.

**What if I already have a logo and want the rest?**

I can build everything around your existing mark. Just send the vector files and I'll assess whether the logo can be built on or needs fixing first. A mini brand book for an existing logo on its own costs 590 PLN.

**How long does preparing a brand identity take?**

I deliver the Marka Start package in 7–10 working days from the deposit; for a wider scope I agree the timing individually. What usually takes longest is approving the logo direction; the rest develops quickly after that.

**Does a brand identity make sense for a one-person business?**

It does, just on a smaller scale. For a sole trader a logo, two colours, one typeface and a business card are enough. Everything else you add when it starts being needed.
