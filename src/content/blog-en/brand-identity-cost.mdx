A logo isn't a brand. A brand is what people think of your business, and a brand identity is the set of rules that makes them see the same thing every time: on a business card, in an email, on your van and on Facebook.

**How much does a brand identity cost?** On the Polish market a basic set (logo, colours, typography, a few items and a brand book) is 1,500–4,500 PLN. With me the Marka Start package (logo, mini brand book, 150 printed business cards and an email signature) costs 1,490 PLN net, plus 23% VAT.

Below is what exactly goes into it, how it compares with the market and where to start if you can't afford everything at once.

## What does a brand identity include?

**Core elements:**
- Logo: the symbol and the name, in horizontal, vertical, colour and one-colour versions.
- Colours: main and supporting, with HEX, CMYK and Pantone codes.
- Typefaces: for headings and for body text, with rules for using them.
- Layout: where things go on your materials and how much space to leave around the logo.

**Items and applications:**
- Business card
- Letterhead and email signature
- Company folder and quote template
- Presentation templates
- Post templates for Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn
- Packaging, if you have any
- Shop sign, roll-up banner, premises signage

**Brand book:**
- A PDF document with rules for using the logo
- Examples of correct and incorrect use
- The colour palette with codes
- Typefaces and typesetting rules

## Why a small business needs it

It's not about prestige. It's about customers not having to wonder whether they're dealing with the same business.

A consistent look:
- Builds trust before you even start talking
- Shortens the work on every new material, because you have ready templates and colours
- Sets you apart from competitors who change their look with every material
- Makes outsourcing easier: the print shop and designer get ready guidelines instead of guessing

<Callout type="info">
The simplest test: put your business card, a printed quote and your Facebook profile picture side by side. If the colours don't match and the logo is in three different proportions, you don't need a new logo, you need rules.
</Callout>

## What drives the cost of a brand identity for a small business?

What a brand identity costs depends on a few things you can name before the first call. Know them and it's easier to compare two quotes, and you won't pay for things you don't need.

**How much needs to be made.** A logo on its own is a different job from a logo, brand book, business cards and email signature. Each item is a separate piece of design. That's why my prices go up in steps: logo with a mini brand book, then Marka Start with business cards, then the package with a website.

**How many changes along the way.** Revisions to the chosen direction are included, and we keep going until you approve. It only costs more if, after picking a direction, you want to start over with a different idea. That's a new project, so it gets a new quote. It's also why I ask a lot of questions at the start: a good brief saves you money. I explain how to write one in [how to write a design brief](/en/blog/how-to-write-a-design-brief).

**Your industry and where the logo will appear.** A clinic needs business cards and a graphic for its Google profile. A garage needs a mark you can read from the street, on the sign and on the van. A business with its own product needs labels and packaging. The more places and materials, the more checking that the mark looks right everywhere.

**Which files you get.** Read quotes carefully here. Some cheap offers hand over a single PNG. Then the print shop sends it back and the sign maker charges extra to redraw it as a vector. With me the full set of formats is included: SVG, PDF, PNG, JPG and source files. What each one is for, I cover in [logo files from a designer](/en/blog/logo-files-from-designer).

## A cheap logo from a generator: where the extra costs show up

A logo from a generator for a few dozen złoty looks like a saving until it goes to print. You usually get one image, with no version for dark backgrounds and no vector file. You pay extra for the business cards, the sign, the T-shirts. Each time someone different fixes the file their own way, so your materials end up looking different anyway. And consistency is the whole point of a brand identity.

I go into this in more detail, with examples, in [AI logo or a graphic designer](/en/blog/ai-logo-or-graphic-designer).

## How work on a brand identity goes

1. **Conversation and brief:** what you do, who you talk to, what sets you apart
2. **Research:** what the competition looks like, so your mark isn't the third identical one
3. **Logo design:** directions to choose from, then refining one
4. **Development:** colours, typefaces, application on materials
5. **Brand book:** the full set of files and usage rules
6. **Handover:** every format: SVG, PDF, PNG, JPG and source files

## FAQ

**How much does a brand identity for a small business cost?**

On the market 1,500–4,500 PLN for a basic set, an extended identity 4,500–9,000 PLN. With me a logo with a mini brand book costs 1,190 PLN net, and the Marka Start package with business cards and an email signature 1,490 PLN net. Both plus 23% VAT.

**Do I need everything at once?**

No. You can start with a logo and build up the identity gradually. What matters is settling at least the colours and typography at the start: they're the foundation of consistency.

**What if I already have a logo and want the rest?**

I can build everything around your existing mark. Just send the vector files and I'll assess whether the logo can be built on or needs fixing first. A mini brand book for an existing logo on its own costs 590 PLN net.

**Can I order just a logo, without a brand book?**

Yes. A logo in three versions (horizontal, vertical and one-colour) starts at 890 PLN net. You can add a mini brand book later for 590 PLN net. If you know a shop sign or van wrap is coming soon, it's cheaper to get the logo with a mini brand book straight away for 1,190 PLN net. All prices plus 23% VAT.

**How long does preparing a brand identity take?**

I deliver the Marka Start package in 7–10 working days from the deposit; for a wider scope I agree the timing individually. What usually takes longest is approving the logo direction; the rest develops quickly after that.

**Does a brand identity make sense for a one-person business?**

It does, just on a smaller scale. For a sole trader a logo, two colours, one typeface and a business card are enough. Everything else you add when it starts being needed.
